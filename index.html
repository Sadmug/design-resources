Mug's Graphic Design Bookmarks

Tools

This is a list of design tools I've collected over the years for graphic design, typography, and digital art. I use these resources for my own work and thought they might be useful to others as well.

Fonts :

  • All of these foundries provide free for comercial use licensed fonts.
  • The League of Moveable Type One of the first free, open-source web font foundries, small library but well-crafted.
  • Collletttivo Open-source, collectively designed contemporary Italian typefaces.
  • Google Fonts Free and open-source web font library, huge but hit-or-miss on quality, but includes a a large collection of Icons.
  • FontShare Curated free fonts from Indian Type Foundry, generally more polished than most free libraries.
  • Velvetyne French, community-run foundry, leans experimental and multilingual.
  • Bye Bye Binary French collective distributing inclusive, non-binary and post-binary typefaces.
  • Open Source Publishing Brussels-based collective building libre fonts and tools, DIY and workshop-driven.

Rasters :

  • Texture Labs A wide range of free high-quality textures.
  • Transparent Textures Free textures for your UI/UX works.
  • Public Domain Image Archive Hand picked collection of out-of-copyright works.
  • Poly Haven Mainly for 3D work but they offer a wide range of textures that can be used for other purposes.
  • Ambient CG Free CC0 PBR materials, more focused purely on textures than Poly Haven.
  • NYPL Digital Collections Collection of items digitized from The New York Public Library's collections.
  • Mockups Design A wide range of free high-quality mockup templates for various design projects.

Color :

  • Adobe Color Color palette generator and library, with a large community of user-submitted palettes.
  • Coolors Fast color palette generator, with a large library of user-submitted palettes.
  • Color Hunt Curated collection of color palettes, with a focus on modern and trendy color combinations.
  • Colormind AI-powered color palette generator, with a focus on generating palettes based on user input.
  • Color Hex All-in-one color tool with a vast collection of colors and coordination.
  • Huemint Color palette generator with templates to visualize color combinations in real use-cases.

Inspirations :

  • are.na Algorith-free platform for curating and sharing ideas.
  • Cosmos Visual moodboarding tool, built around a searchable image grid with a color based search tool.
  • Behance Adobe's portfolio platform, full project case studies across every design discipline.
  • Pinterest The default moodboard tool, deep library but mid algorithm and ad driven.
  • Branding Styleguides archive of real brand guideline documents pulled from actual companies.
  • Fonts in Use Archive of typefaces spotted in real design work, searchable by font or by industry.
  • Savee Minimalist, ad-free image curation, another alternative to Pinterest, similar to Cosmos.

Software :

  • FontForge Free, open-source editor for building or modifying typefaces at the point/curve level.
  • Graphite (Web based) Early-stage open-source vector editor, node-based and non-destructive.
  • Photopea (Web based) Photoshop alternative, opens PSD and other native formats.
  • VectorPea (Web based) Illustrator alternative from the same developer as Photopea, opens AI, SVG and PDF files.
  • JamPea (Web based) MIDI editor from the same developer as Photopea, also imports Guitar Pro and MusicXML files.
  • Inkscape Free, open-source vector editor, similar to Illustrator.
  • Krita Free, open-source raster editor, focused on digital painting and illustration.
  • GIMP Free, open-source raster editor, similar to Photoshop.
  • RawTherapee free, open-source RAW photo processor.
  • Affinity (Requires a Canva account) Professional creative suite for design and photo editing, free alternative to Photoshop, Illustrator & Indesign, with canva AI integration.
  • Canva (Web based) Simple and fast design tool with a vast library of templates and assets.
  • Wordmark types your text out across every font installed on your system at once, for quick comparison.
  • PureRef lightweight reference board, keeps pinned images on top of other windows.
  • Aseprite (This is a pre-build of Aseprite for Microslop Windows, you can can find the source code for Aseprite here and build it yourself for free, or you can buy a one-time paid key here to support the developer)
  • AnyGradient (Web based) universal Gradient Generator with OKLCH & Display-P3 support.
